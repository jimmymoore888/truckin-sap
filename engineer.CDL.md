## Originating Proposition

As automated and self\-driving commercial vehicles assume more of the dynamic driving task, the human professional responsible for a Class A commercial vehicle may evolve from a conventional driver into a safety\-critical transportation\-systems operator\.

Jimmy W\. Moore proposes that the United States develop a federally uniform **Engineering\-Class CDL\-A Autonomous Commercial Vehicle Systems Operator Credential** built on the existing Class A CDL framework\.

The credential would formally recognize qualified CDL\-A professionals who are trained and authorized to supervise, activate, monitor, diagnose, intervene in, recover, and document automated commercial\-motor\-vehicle operations within defined safety and operational limits\.

## Proposed FMCSA Framework

Jimmy W\. Moore proposes that the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration &#40;FMCSA&#41;, working within the U\.S\. Department of Transportation and in coordination with state driver\-licensing agencies, develop a national standard containing the following elements:

1. **Universal CDL\-A pathway\.** Every active Class A CDL holder may qualify, with or without additional endorsements and regardless of whether the driver has participated in the SAP process\.
2. **Tenure and competency\.** Engineering\-class recognition would follow a defined period of verified CDL\-A service, continued qualification, an acceptable safety record, and demonstrated technical competency\.
3. **Endorsements as specialties\.** Existing endorsements would serve as specialty qualifications within the broader framework\. Lack of a particular endorsement would not disqualify a driver from the base credential\.
4. **Autonomous\-systems authority\.** Additional training and examination would cover automated\-driving\-system limitations, system monitoring, control transfer, minimal\-risk stops, incident response, remote supervision, cybersecurity awareness, and event documentation\.
5. **Objective recognition\.** Once the uniform requirements are satisfied, the designation would follow through an objective administrative process and would not depend on a carrier’s preferred job title\.
6. **Experienced\-driver transition\.** A legacy pathway would give experienced CDL\-A professionals a fair route into the automated\-vehicle era rather than treating automation as eliminating their professional authority\.

## SAP/Return\-to\-Duty Experience as the Conception Catalyst

Jimmy W\. Moore states that he completed the DOT SAP/RTD process and subsequently maintained approximately four years and six months of continuing qualification and compliance within the regulated commercial\-driving system\. He identifies that prolonged period of heightened scrutiny, documented responsibility, and continued safety\-sensitive eligibility as the catalyst for this proposal\.

Under the proposed framework:

- prior SAP participation would **not be required** for engineering\-class recognition;
- the underlying violation would **not be rewarded**;
- successful RTD completion and documented sustained post\-return compliance could count as evidence of regulatory discipline and professional resilience;
- drivers without SAP history would qualify through the ordinary tenure\-and\-competency pathway; and
- every candidate would be required to meet the same technical and safety standard\.

Jimmy W\. Moore proposes that his approximately four years and six months of sustained post\-return qualification be eligible to satisfy the regulatory\-discipline component of an experienced\-driver pathway, subject to documentary verification and the same technical\-competency requirements applied to other candidates\.

## Proposed Title and Legal Boundary

The working title is **Engineering\-Class Commercial Vehicle Systems Operator**\. A future statute or regulation could authorize the title **Commercial Transportation Systems Engineer** for persons meeting the new standard\.

This is a proposed future credential\. A current CDL\-A is not a Professional Engineer license, and this proposal does not represent that Jimmy W\. Moore or any other CDL holder is presently licensed to practice professional engineering or to sign and seal engineering plans\.
