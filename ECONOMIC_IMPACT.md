# Central Economic Question

The economic issue presented for attorney review is not whether I subjectively believe that I "deserve" higher compensation.

The issue is objectively measurable:

**Would I probably have obtained materially higher-paying commercial-driving employment but for the allegedly unlawful historical-SAP hiring exclusion?**

If so:

**What would I probably have earned compared with what I actually earned?**

That difference is the appropriate starting point for evaluating potential economic loss.
